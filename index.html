01 / ABOUT

About me

My research focuses on reliable communication, routing protocols, and the Internet of Things.

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Engineering at Manipal Institute of Technology, MAHE University, Manipal. My academic and research interests include reliable communication, routing protocols, and the Internet of Things.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati under the supervision of Dr. Manas Khatua. I received my M.Tech. degree in Computer Science and Engineering from the National Institute of Technology Rourkela and my B.Tech. degree in Computer Science and Engineering from the International Institute of Information Technology, Bhubaneswar.

My research interests include the Internet of Things (IoT), Wireless Sensor Networks, and routing protocols for mission-critical IoT applications. I am also interested in interdisciplinary research in Cyber-Physical Systems, Edge Computing for IoT, Industry 4.0 networks, AI/ML-driven network optimization, and secure and resilient communication systems.
