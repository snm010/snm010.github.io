ABOUT ME

A little about me

I work on dependable communication and routing protocols for low-power and lossy networks supporting mission-critical Internet of Things applications.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati. I hold an M.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from the National Institute of Technology Rourkela and a B.Tech. from the International Institute of Information Technology, Bhubaneswar.

My research focuses on reliable, delay-efficient and energy-aware multi-path routing, with broader interests in wireless sensor networks, industrial IoT, edge computing and intelligent networked systems.
