RESEARCH PROJECTS

Selected projects

Selected research and development projects from doctoral, postgraduate and student work.

Smart Wheelchair Management System

Guided M.Tech. students at IIT Guwahati for an IoT course project (August–October 2024). Features included wheelchair location detection, occupancy monitoring and alert notifications. Components included NodeMCU and RFID reader.

IoT-Based Smart Anti-Theft System

Project developed during October–November 2020 under the guidance of Dr. Manas Khatua. Included data acquisition, communication, image capture and face-detection modules, using Arduino UNO, GSM, PIR sensor, Python and ThingSpeak.

Study of Routing Protocols for Low-Power IoT Devices

M.Tech. research thesis (July 2017–June 2020), under Dr. Suchismita Chinara. Focused on scalability and energy efficiency in IoT routing protocols; tools included NS-2 and COOJA/Contiki OS.

Adaptive Routing in LLN Networks for Mission-Critical IoT Applications

Ph.D. research (July 2020–January 2026), under Dr. Manas Khatua. Focused on hard reliability and bounded latency for mission-critical IoT applications using COOJA/Contiki OS.
